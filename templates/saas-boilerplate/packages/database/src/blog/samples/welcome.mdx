## Why we started this blog

This is where we share what we are building, why we build it that way and what we learn along the way. Expect three kinds of posts:

- **Product updates**: what changed in a release and how it affects you.
- **Guides**: step-by-step walkthroughs for common tasks.
- **Lessons learned**: the decisions behind the product, including the ones we got wrong.

## How to follow along

- Bookmark the blog page; the newest posts are always at the top.
- Subscribe to the RSS feed in your favourite reader.
- Filter by tag to see only the topics you care about.

## Where to go next

If you are looking for reference material rather than stories, the documentation covers every feature in depth.
